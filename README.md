# Armed Forces of Nogova

## Overview

This project aims to introduce a new faction to ArmA: Armed Assault: the Armed Forces of Nogova, or AFN for short.

## Project Status

This project is still in a very early stage of development and is subject to significant changes.

Originally, the mod was intended to introduce the Everon Defense Force (EDF). However, after some consideration, I decided that Nogova would be a better setting for the Eastern European-inspired military I have in mind.

A lot of assets still carry the EDF naming convention and will be renamed gradually as development progresses.

## Features

- Three distinct military components:
  - Territorial Defense Forces (TDF)
  - Land Forces (LF)
  - Special Operations Forces (SOF)
- Each component will have its own uniforms and equipment philosophy.
- Dedicated missions will showcase each component.
- AK models equipped with CQB optics (Kobra).
- Hybrid optics:
  - Medium-range optics such as the ACOG will feature a small red dot sight mounted on top.
  - Players will be able to switch between the two optics, allowing them to engage targets at both medium and close range.

Additionally, the Armed Forces of Nogova will have a hopefully believable background story. I want every design decision to make sense within the context of a small island nation and its military.

Much of this will be background lore, intended to give the AFN an authentic feeling, but it will also play a role in future missions.

The mod is being developed for vanilla ArmA: Armed Assault, with ACE compatibility planned for a later stage.
